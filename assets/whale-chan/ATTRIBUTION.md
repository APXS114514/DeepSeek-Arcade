## What we used, and what we changed

Every file below is a **derived** work: DeepSeek Arcade never ships the upstream
PNGs. The derivation pipeline is identical for all of them:

1. **Edge-connected background removal** — the near-white backdrop is flood-filled
   from the image border and keyed to transparency. It is connected-region based,
   not a global white-key, so the character's white lab coat is preserved.
2. **Alpha feathering** — a soft ~1px alpha ramp where the matte meets the edge.
3. **Frame selection** — 9 of the 21 upstream `character/` PNGs are used; the rest
   (pose variants, hover-wave strip, reference sheet) are not redistributed.
4. **Cropping** — each frame is cropped to its alpha bounding box; the head icons
   are additionally cropped to a square face region.
5. **Uniform rescaling** — all full-body frames share **one** scale factor so their
   relative sizes stay correct, then are bottom-centred on a 300×340 canvas.
6. **WebP encoding** — lossy WebP, quality 90, with an alpha channel.

### Modifications summary (for CC BY 4.0 §3(a)(1)(B))

> Background removed by edge-connected flood fill, alpha feathered, cropped to the
> subject (and to a square face region for the head icons), uniformly rescaled,
> bottom-centred on a fixed transparent canvas, and re-encoded as lossy WebP
> (quality 90) with an alpha channel. Nine frames were selected out of the upstream
> `character/` set; no pixels were drawn or generated by DeepSeek Arcade, and no
> colour grading was applied.
